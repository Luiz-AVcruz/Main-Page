About me

I like tinkering with the tools I use every day. I use Linux, and when I feel like something’s missing from the way I track my study time or finances, I sometimes end up building my own tool.

I started making mods to solve problems that came up while playing with friends. I enjoy figuring out what’s missing, building a solution, and trying it in-game to see if it makes things easier for the people playing.
