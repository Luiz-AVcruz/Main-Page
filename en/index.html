About me

I started building mods around things I wanted in the games I played with friends. When I wanted a better way to organize our Teardown sessions, I built a panel to bring the controls together and delegate tasks inside the game.

I work on both the interface and the rules behind it. That means deciding who can perform each action, what needs to stay saved, and how the tool fits into the game’s existing systems.
